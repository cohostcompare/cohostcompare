Add it to Gmail

  1. Click Copy signature. If nothing copies, select everything inside the white box with your mouse and press Cmd+C (Ctrl+C on Windows).
  2. In Gmail, open Settings (gear) → See all settings → General → Signature → Create new, name it "CoHostCompare".
  3. Click in the signature box and paste (Cmd+V). The logo and links come across.
  4. Under Signature defaults, set it for new emails and for replies/forwards, then Save changes at the bottom.
